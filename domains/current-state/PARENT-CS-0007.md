## Summary
The current platform creates recurring cost pressure through annual ETL-tool
licensing and on-premises warehouse hardware maintenance.

## Details
ETL licensing and warehouse hardware maintenance are recurring annual budget
items. The cost pressure is an ongoing financial concern in addition to the
platform's technical constraints.

## Impact
Modernization decisions must account for recurring licensing and maintenance
costs as well as scalability and delivery limitations.
