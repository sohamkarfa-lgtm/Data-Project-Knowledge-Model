## Statement
The platform shall provide cost visibility to Finance from the beginning of
the initial delivery milestone.

## Rationale
Finance needs usable consumption data to understand and forecast cloud costs
before usage is scaled across all four business domains.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Cost tracking is available from week one of Milestone 1.
- Finance can access the relevant cost information.
- Dev/test usage is capped during the initial milestone.
