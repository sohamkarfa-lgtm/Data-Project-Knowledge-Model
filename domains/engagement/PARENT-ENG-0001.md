## Summary
MNC Pvt Ltd wants to modernize its Data & Analytics platform from an on-premises
estate to a cloud-based platform, to improve scalability, reduce operational
overhead, and enable AI-assisted analytics.

## Details
The organization currently runs analytics and reporting on an on-premises data
warehouse and a set of scheduled ETL jobs maintained by a small central team.
Growth in data volume, increasing number of downstream reporting consumers, and
rising infrastructure maintenance cost have made the current model unsustainable.

Sponsor: Chief Data Officer.
Target horizon: modernization program to deliver incremental value every
quarter rather than a single "big bang" cutover.

Success is defined as: new business use cases can be onboarded onto the
platform without a multi-month infrastructure lead time, and the platform can
scale storage/compute independently of each other.

## Open Questions
- Is there a hard deadline driven by on-prem hardware/license end-of-life?
- What is the appetite for parallel-running old and new platforms during
  transition (cost vs. risk trade-off)?
